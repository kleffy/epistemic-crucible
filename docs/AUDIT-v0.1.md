# v0.1 record audit

The `v0.1-arxiv` tag and its artifacts are frozen for reproduction. They must not
be read as evidence that an evaluated policy possessed internal causal knowledge.

The v0.1 colour diagnostic reported `0.57`, exactly the observed rate at which a
red tool was available (`0.57/0.60` in the relevant runs). It therefore does not
identify saturation or a red-controlled policy. The v0.2 manuscript and software
correct four additional reporting points:

- the reasoning path allowed 3,000 completion tokens;
- the relevant inference used low reasoning effort;
- the retained context was nine messages, not nine turns;
- the released learned checkpoint was trained with behavior cloning, despite the
  legacy `neural_ppo` module name.

The five v0.1 families remain available only for reproduction. The four families
outside the new conductivity assay are excluded from primary v0.2 claims until
their construct issues are repaired separately.
